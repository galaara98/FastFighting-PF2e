# FastFighting PF2e

> **AI use:** This module used AI in its creation.
> - **Programming:** I am a skilled programmer by profession, but AI was used heavily as a co-pilot.
> - **Strings:** ALL initial strings were proposed by a LLM to get the concepts out, ALL strings were  tracked to make sure they were re-written (for simple or ttrpg/game specific phrases, approved by) a native speaker.

Pathfinder Second Edition support for [FastFighting Core](https://github.com/galaara98/FastFighting), the Foundry VTT battle estimator, storyteller and resolver.

## What it does

FastFighting predicts how a fight will go, plays the creatures you don't want to run by hand, and writes every turn back onto the sheets. This module teaches it PF2e, by the remaster: three actions and the multiple attack penalty, four degrees of success, conditions, maneuvers and grabs, spells and slots, Focus Points and consumables, summons and companions, class features, creature abilities and their recharges, reactions, dying, wounded and Hero Points.

It finds features by slug, so a translated game plays the same.

## Why you'd want it

- **Before the fight:** see whether an encounter is trivial or deadly before your players find out.
- **During it:** let FastFighting run the monsters, or anyone whose player is away, rolled with PF2e's own cards and applied PF2e's way.
- **After it:** Rewind a turn or the whole encounter, sheets and chat included.

[How PF2e plays](docs/how-pf2e-plays.md) has every rule, setting and what isn't played.

## Requirements

- Foundry v14
- The `pf2e` system, 8.0 or later
- FastFighting Core 0.17.0 or later
